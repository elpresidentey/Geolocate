Orbital Freight — Earth-View Shipping Console

A mission-view of global ocean-freight traffic, lit by a single Sun whose direction is derived from UTC — solar longitude in the ecliptic, declination from the exact spherical relation, and the equation of time applied. Orbit the globe, read the terminator, and inspect the world's major trade lanes — from the Strait of Malacca to the Panama Canal.

Values on this page are drawn from the sources listed above. Anything that cannot be sourced is shown as unavailable rather than estimated. There is deliberately no distance-based "risk" rating — real maritime risk is chokepoint- and weather-specific, and none of the sources here serve it. Route ETAs are a great-circle distance divided by a stated 17-knot service speed (756 km/day) — a planning figure, not a booking.

Drag to orbit · scroll to zoom · use the focus menu to follow a body · click a lane to isolate it · click a vessel row to fly to its reported position. Built with three.js r160 and public NASA texture sets. This is not a NASA product; "NASA" refers only to the origin of the texture data.
